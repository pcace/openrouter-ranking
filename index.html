Methodology

Cost is calculated as (prompt × 750k) + (completion × 250k) per 1M tokens, assuming a typical 3:1 prompt-to-completion ratio. Data is fetched from the OpenRouter API in real time. Free and router-only models are excluded. Ranking prioritizes value — not just cheapest, not just best quality, but the intersection of both.
